# GreenBody V49 working baseline

V49 is an unfinished, verifiable geometry baseline, SHA-256
`904b729858ac765cc53866a7e100a90fef628953c8bd45fe9283269bc83374bd`.

It adds only a 6 mm smooth shoulder-root X inset to V47. All relative shape
keys receive the same rest-space displacement; rig, weights, poses, cameras,
material and scoring are unchanged. Its v4 scores are front 81.75468, side
85.07590 and back 81.64415; mean 82.82491, minimum 81.64415. This improves the
selection's aggregate mean/minimum versus V47, although side is 0.03835 lower.

`renders/v49-experiments/shoulder-root-inset-06/` contains structural and
fresh driver QA plus inspected squat/push-up renders. This is not 99 and not
animation/runtime approval.
