REPDUO · PRIVACY

Private by design.

Effective 21 August 2026

Camera processing

RepDuo uses the iPhone camera to detect body position and count squats and push-ups. Camera frames, person masks, and pose points are processed on the device. The P0 app does not record, retain, upload, or share them.

Workout data

RepDuo stores workout summaries such as movement type, rep count, duration, date, and tracking-quality events locally on the iPhone. P0 has no account, advertising, analytics, or cloud-sync service.

Permissions

Live counting requires Camera access. RepDuo does not request Microphone or Health access in P0. You can change Camera access in iOS Settings and clear local workout history inside RepDuo Settings.

Optional devices

RepDuo may display the current system audio route and public Apple Watch pairing, installation, and reachability status. P0 does not store device identifiers or claim AirPods battery information.

Changes and contact

This policy will be updated before new data features are enabled. The publisher must add its legal name, jurisdiction, support email, and final HTTPS policy address before App Store submission.
